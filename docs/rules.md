# Philippine accounting rules

The rules of filament-storybook-ph, which layers Philippine VAT, withholding, TINs and BIR forms on the core patterns. They follow the core rulebook (`docs/rules.md` in filament-storybook) and its policy: a UI rule needs a source, and a business fact comes from the owner.

## Rules

- **What staff copy onto a paper form is read-only text in the form's own order, each label beside its value (Carbon structured list), each value copyable,** with an amount also in words as it is written on a receipt. Carbon read-only states (Aug 2026: read-only text stays navigable and can be copied). *Pattern `records-paper-receipt`.*
- **VAT is worked out once per invoice for each VAT class** (12%, zero-rated, exempt), on that class's total, then rounded to 2 decimals, and shown as a separate item with taxable, zero-rated and exempt sales broken out (BIR RR 7-2024). *Pattern `forms-document-totals-with-vat`.*
- **A report shows the VAT-exclusive amount, the VAT and the total in their own columns** (owner, 2026-10-12). *Patterns `reports-*-with-vat`, `reports-collections-with-withholding`.*
- **Recording a supplier's invoice asks the user to confirm it is a proper VAT invoice** (the supplier's TIN, "VAT-registered", the VAT on its own line), and warns above the buttons, without blocking, when the printed VAT is more than ₱0.05 from 12/112 of the total; the warning is announced (`role="status"`), since it answers what was just typed. Carbon notification (Sep 2026: a warning callout "above the relevant button or input"); WCAG 4.1.3. *Pattern `forms-document-totals.supplierInvoice`.*
- **Withholding has its quarterly screens:** tax withheld from suppliers, one row per supplier and ATC with each month of the quarter as BIR's Form 2307 shows them, "Download 2307 details" on each row and the QAP as an export; tax withheld by customers (the SAWT), claimed in the quarter paid, with the payments whose 2307 hasn't arrived listed to collect, and a gray "Mark 2307 received" on a payment. Neither export is BIR's DAT file; the CPA loads them into BIR's alphalist tool. *Patterns `reports-tax-withheld-from-suppliers`, `reports-tax-withheld-by-customers`, `actions-mark-2307-received`.*

## Business standards (from the owner)

- **TIN:** the 9-digit TIN plus a 3-digit branch code, stored as `000-000-000-000`, with `000` for the head office. *Pattern `forms-tin`.*
- **Phone numbers:** Philippine mobile and landline numbers only, typed any way, **stored as +63 917 123 4567, +63 2 8123 4567 (Metro Manila) or +63 32 123 4567 (provinces)** (owner, 2026-10-12). *Pattern `forms-contact-details.phone`, `PhoneNumber`.*
- **Sales invoices and collection receipts are written by hand on BIR pre-printed forms,** which are the official copies. The app keeps the records (it is not registered as a computerized accounting system), and each is recorded under the serial printed on its form: the user types it when posting, and the app refuses a serial already recorded. One number per document, so the books, the forms and BIR's records agree. Staff copy a collection receipt from its View page. *Patterns `records-document-lifecycle`, `actions-record-payment`, `records-paper-receipt`.*
- **Prices include VAT.** VAT is 12/112 of each document's VATable total, worked out once and rounded once, half up; the VATable sales are that total less the VAT. *Pattern `forms-document-totals`.*
- **Withholding tax (EWT) applies both ways.** A sales invoice's total amount due is before withholding.
  - **From a customer:** what they withhold depends on who they are, set on the customer: a **government office** withholds 1% income tax and 5% VAT on the VAT-exclusive amount, with no income tax on a single purchase of ₱10,000 or less (RA 10963; each invoice's total is the purchase, confirmed by the CPA on 2026-10-12), a **top withholding agent** 1% income tax, others nothing. A collection proposes both and the cash expected (`DocumentTotals::customerRates()`), and the user records what the customer's 2307 shows. Both are recorded with the payment as creditable taxes, even before the 2307 arrives, each with its ATC, and **claimed in the quarter the payment was received, whether or not the 2307 is in yet** (owner, 2026-10-18, replacing 2026-10-12's "only once the 2307 is in hand"; RR 2-98 Sec. 2.58.3 credits the tax in the quarter the income was received, and waiting made the SAWT and the 2550Q disagree), with each 2307 still collected to support the claim; they are kept apart: the income tax is credited on the income tax return, the VAT withheld (WV010) on the VAT return only, since BIR forfeits VAT withheld claimed on the income tax return (RMC 36-2021; RA 10963). The payment keeps the date the 2307 arrived, and the app lists payments still waiting for one. *Pattern `actions-record-payment`.*
  - **To a supplier:** worked out once, when the supplier's invoice is recorded against the purchase order, since RR 4-2024 makes withholding due when the amount is recorded or the invoice is issued, whichever comes first: the rate of the supplier's ATC (a default on the supplier, changeable on the invoice) on the invoice's total less VAT. Payments settle the net payable. One BIR Form 2307 per supplier per quarter, covering all of that quarter's withholding, issued after the quarter ends (or for a payment, if the supplier asks) (owner, 2026-10-12, replacing one per purchase order). A withholding falls in the month its supplier's invoice was recorded (RR 4-2024). The app gives each supplier's quarter as "Form 2307 details" to copy onto BIR's form, and the quarter's QAP as a CSV or Excel file. The supplier's TIN and the ATC are kept with each withholding. WC158 (1% on goods) and WC160 (2% on services) apply only to a company BIR has made a top withholding agent, which the app's `config/company.php` says (`top_withholding_agent`); one of the owner's companies is. Rent, contractors and professional fees have their own ATCs either way, added as the CPA confirms them. *Pattern `forms-document-totals.supplierInvoice`.*
- **The companies trade goods** (owner, 2026-10-12): a government customer withholds the goods rate, 1% income tax; the services rate doesn't arise. The withholding codes (ATCs) and their rates are a table the business keeps up to date as the CPA confirms them, not codes written into the app.
- **The invoice is the main document** for every sale (RA 11976, the Ease of Paying Taxes Act; RR 7-2024): official receipts are only supplementary (owner, 2026-10-12).
- **Alphalists** (owner, 2026-10-12): the app produces each quarter's QAP (the suppliers we withheld from, for the 1601-EQ) and SAWT (the tax customers withheld from us, in the quarter it was paid, whether or not the 2307 is in yet; owner, 2026-10-18, following RR 2-98 Sec. 2.58.3, so the SAWT and the 2550Q agree), for the CPA to file.
- **A supplier's invoice is recorded as printed:** its total and its VAT as the supplier wrote them, since the printed VAT supports the input tax; the app shows its own figure beside it to catch typing mistakes. Before it is recorded, the user confirms it is a proper VAT invoice (the supplier's TIN, "VAT-registered", and VAT shown separately), since input VAT on an invoice without them can be disallowed; and when the printed VAT differs from 12/112 of the total by more than ₱0.05, the app warns, so a wrong invoice is sent back for correction rather than claimed (owner, 2026-10-12).
- **A supplier's invoice is recorded on its purchase order,** not as a document of its own (owner, 2026-10-11): the order holds the supplier's invoice number, date and printed figures, its withholding and its payments. *Patterns `forms-document-totals.supplierInvoice`, `records-view-page`.*

**Decided by the owner (2026-10-10):**
1. **VAT is worked out per invoice for each VAT class,** not per line. Confirmed by the CPA on 2026-10-12.
2. **None of the owner's companies is under BIR's e-invoicing (EIS) mandate at present** (RR 11-2025, RMC 98-2026). If one comes under it, EIS's technical specification sets field precision and overrides this section.

**For the CPA** (questions prepared 2026-10-11): whether payments for rent, professional fees or contractor services (which a goods trader still makes, and which carry their own withholding) will be recorded in the app, and if so their ATCs and rates; and whether RR 24-2025 changes WC158 and WC160. The accounting app's research (2026-10-09) reads RR 24-2025 and RMO 46-2025 as adding WC840, WC850 and WC860 (0.5% on wholesale purchases of motor vehicles, medicines and fuels from manufacturers and direct importers, from 10 October 2025) beside them, leaving WC158 at 1% and WC160 at 2%; that is research, not yet the CPA's answer. BIR registration: no change for now (owner, 2026-10-12); the paper forms stay the official invoices and receipts, and the app's PDFs stay copies.
